Privacy Policy for Slate

Application: Slate — Smart Board & Interactive Whiteboard for Schools (com.slate.whiteboard)
Developer / Data Fiduciary: SNANS TECH

Core Privacy Principles:
  • Zero Ads & Zero Tracking: Slate contains zero third-party advertising, zero ad SDKs, and zero behavioral trackers.
  • Offline-First: Whiteboards, drawings, and lesson materials remain strictly local on the classroom device unless explicitly saved or synced by an authenticated teacher.
  • Children's Privacy Protection: Designed in strict adherence with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Section 9) and Google Play's Families Policy.

1. India's DPDP Act, 2023 Compliance (Children's Personal Data)

In accordance with Section 9 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • No Detrimental Processing: Slate strictly avoids any processing of personal data that is likely to cause any detrimental effect on the well-being of a child.
  • No Tracking or Profiling: We do not conduct behavioral monitoring, user activity tracking, or targeted advertising directed at children or students.
  • Educational Setting Authorization: In classroom environments, student interactive participation (such as quizzes or polls) occurs under the direct supervision of educators and schools acting with lawful institutional authority.
  • Data Principal Rights: Users, educators, and guardians have statutory rights to access personal data summaries, demand correction or erasure of information, and lodge grievances with our Grievance Officer.

2. Google Play Families Policy Compliance

  • Target Audience: Designed for classrooms, students (K-12), and educators of all age groups.
  • Zero Advertising IDs: We do not transmit or access Android Advertising Identifiers (AAID) or permanent device serial numbers.
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: Slate contains no tracking, marketing, or profiling SDKs.

3. Hardware Permissions & Sensitive Data Use

A. Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

Strictly User-Initiated: The camera is accessed exclusively when an educator or student taps an explicit action button:

  • Document & Homework Scanning: Capturing physical textbook exercises, handwritten diagrams, or student work to project onto the whiteboard.
  • Optical Formula & Handwriting OCR: Capturing written mathematical equations to convert into editable LaTeX formulas or vector strokes.
  • QR Pairing: Scanning teacher remote codes or shared whiteboard links.

Guarantees: No background camera access. No facial recognition or biometric indexing. Video frames are never streamed silently or stored on third-party servers.

B. Microphone Permission (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

Ephemerally Processed: Used solely for:

  • Real-Time Classroom Captions: Live speech-to-text to assist hearing-impaired students or support multilingual learning during lectures.
  • Voice-Activated Controls: Hands-free tool switching on the smartboard.

Guarantees: Audio is converted to text in real time on the device. Raw audio recordings are never recorded to disk, never stored, and never retained on servers.

4. Interactive Classroom Features & Student Data

A. Student Work Wall (Blackboard Showcase)

Students can upload photographs of their notebook exercises to the board via a temporary QR code session. Submissions are tied to a transient 4-character room code, stored in an access-restricted bucket, and purged automatically at the end of class or during the daily End-of-Day (EOD) cleanup.

B. Live Polls, Quizzes, and Team Buzzers

Students join via a temporary web link without registering an account. No personal information (no email, phone number, or student roll number) is collected. Nicknames are temporary and discarded when the session ends.

C. Educational AI Tools (Gemini Integration)

AI tools (Lesson Builder, Math Solver) operate through secure, authenticated server endpoints. Zero API keys are bundled into client APKs. Prompts are used exclusively to produce educational responses and are never used to train third-party foundation models.

5. Data Security, Cloud Sync & Deletion

  • Local by Default: Boards reside locally on the device storage. Cloud sync is optional and available only to authenticated teachers via encrypted Supabase PostgreSQL storage with Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • School Fleet Auto-Wipe: Schools can enable automated daily closing-time wipes to clear temporary boards and sign out teachers from shared displays.
  • One-Click Account & Data Erasure: Educators can delete their account and all synced cloud whiteboards at any time under Profile & Account → Delete Account & Cloud Data. Deletion is instantaneous and permanent.
